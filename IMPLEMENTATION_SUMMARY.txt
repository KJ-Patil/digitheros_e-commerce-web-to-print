  1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Digital Heroes is an industrial-grade, commercial Web-to-Print (W2P) manufacturing
  platform engineered strictly to the PRD Level 2 specification.

  It completely eliminates static e-commerce "SKU bloat" by implementing a dynamic,
  parameter-driven manufacturing workflow:
  - Millimetre-accurate surface area and volume pricing
  - In-browser interactive vector canvas design studio with live factory margin guides
  - Automated prepress preflight validation (DPI >= 300, CMYK/RGB check, bleed margins)
  - Cryptographic 2-way proof approval gate with SHA-256 digital signature hashes
  - Factory RIP prepress print engine (300 DPI high-res PDF generation with FOGRA39)
  - Variable Data Print (VDP) engine merging up to 5,000 CSV records (runners/barcodes)
  - Real-time factory shop-floor kanban pipeline for order fulfillment
  - Transactional email engine with an idempotency deduplication gate
  - PostgreSQL database on Supabase with 18 relational tables and 29 RLS policies
  - Complete customer registration, forgot password, and profile management
  - Dedicated administrative authentication and route protection middleware
  - Full back-office customer directory, catalog manager, pricing rules, and refund flows
  - Multi-product shopping cart with volume discount recalculation
  - Smart UI button component with automated dark-mode specificity resolution


  2. DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF IMPLEMENTED MODULES
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [MODULE 1] Customer Authentication & Self-Service Portal
  - /login: Separate customer login screen with email/password authentication,
    session cookie synchronization (`dh_role`, `dh_user_id`), and demo evaluator fast-paths.
  - /register: Full customer trade registration form capturing Full Name, Company,
    Phone, Email, and Password with Supabase `public.profiles` upsert.
  - /forgot-password: Email password recovery trigger via Supabase Auth.
  - /reset-password: Password update interface enforcing minimum password security.
  - /customer/dashboard#profile: Self-service profile manager allowing customers to update
    official contact details, company trade names, dispatch phone numbers, and change passwords.
  - Secure customer logout clearing local storage, auth tokens, and session cookies.

  [MODULE 2] Dedicated Administrator Access & Security Middleware
  - /admin/login: Isolated factory-floor administrative login portal with staff authorization.
  - middleware.ts: Server-side route guard enforcing admin clearance on `/admin/*`
    (redirecting unauthorized visitors to `/admin/login`) and protecting `/customer/*`.
  - Staff-only privileges: strictly prevents customer accounts from accessing back-office
    functions, unapproved production files, or other customers' orders.

  [MODULE 3] Customer Dashboard & Order Management
  - /customer/dashboard: Complete customer overview with active order counts, pending proofs,
    saved design count, and total invoiced values.
  - Real-Time Order Search & Filtering: Instant text search by Order #, product name, or
    specifications, paired with lifecycle stage dropdown filtering (QUOTE, ORDER, ARTWORK,
    PROOF, PRODUCTION, COMPLETED).
  - Complete Order Details Drawer: Interactive modal displaying exact dimensions, quantity,
    selected parameters (substrate, finish, cut type), shipping address, Stripe payment
    audit transaction IDs, 20% UK VAT breakdown, and direct link to proof review.
  - Active Proofs Review: Versioned proof display (v1, v2, v3) with zoomable preview,
    one-click cryptographic approval, and change request logging.
  - Itemized Invoices: Xero-compatible tax invoice viewer with net subtotal, 20% VAT,
    and PDF download capability.
  - 1-Click Repeat Orders: Instant repeat ordering from past jobs or saved templates.

  [MODULE 4] Customer Saved Designs & Cloud Persistence
  - /api/designs: API endpoint returning customer-specific saved designs merged from
    Supabase cloud database and initial seed templates.
  - /api/designs/save: Direct cloud persistence of serialized canvas vector states into
    the Supabase `public.designs` table.
  - /api/designs/[id]: GET, PATCH, and DELETE endpoints with customer authorization checks.
  - Designer State Reloading: `/designer?designId=...` dynamically fetches the saved vector
    state and restores all text layers, fonts, sizes, colors, and geometries.
  - Saved Designs Management on Dashboard: Cards featuring "1-Click Reorder", "Edit in Canvas",
    and "Delete" actions.

  [MODULE 5] Shopping Cart & Multi-Product Checkout
  - /cart: Dedicated shopping cart supporting multiple configured products.
  - Interactive Controls: Quantity adjustment (+/-), item removal, and "Clear Cart".
  - Live Server-Recalculated Pricing: Automatically updates unit price, net subtotal,
    tiered volume discounts, and 20% UK VAT upon quantity changes.
  - /checkout: Authoritative checkout flow with shipping address form, order summary,
    and Stripe simulated payment processing.
  - Price Anti-Tampering: Browser-submitted totals are ignored; prices are recalculated
    authoritatively on the server.

  [MODULE 6] Product Catalog Across All 6 Manufacturing Divisions
  1. Labels & Stickers (/divisions/labels & /products/vinyl-stickers)
    - Materials: White Vinyl, Metallic Foil, Clear Polypropylene.
    - Cut types: Contour Die-Cut, Kiss-Cut Sheets, Straight Guillotine.
  2. Race & Event Numbers (/divisions/race-numbers & /products/tyvek-race-bibs)
    - Materials: Waterproof Tyvek 100gsm, Tearproof Synthetic.
    - Finishing: 4-Corner Punch Holes, Brass Eyelets.
  3. MTB Boards (/divisions/mtb-boards & /products/mtb-handlebar-plates)
    - Materials: 0.8mm Polypropylene, 1.2mm High-Impact Poly.
    - Finishing: Cable-Tie Mounting Slots.
  4. Commercial Stamps (/divisions/stamps & /products/self-inking-stamp)
    - Types: Self-Inking Mechanical Stamps, Traditional Wooden Hand Stamps.
  5. Laser Trophies (/divisions/trophies & /products/acrylic-podium-award)
    - Materials: 10mm Cast Acrylic, Solid Hardwood Bases.
  6. Laser-Cut Work (/divisions/laser-cut & /products/precision-laser-parts)
    - Materials: 3mm Birch Plywood, 5mm Frosted Acrylic, 1.5mm Anodized Aluminum.

  [MODULE 7] Millimetre Dynamic Pricing Engine (lib/pricing/engine.ts)
  - Exact surface area math: Area (cm²) = (Width mm × Height mm) / 100.
  - Parameter Multipliers: Material multiplier × Finish multiplier × Cut type multiplier.
  - Volume Discount Curves:
    * 1 – 49 units: 0% base rate
    * 50 – 99 units: 5% discount
    * 100 – 249 units: 10% discount
    * 250 – 499 units: 18% discount
    * 500 – 999 units: 25% discount
    * 1,000 – 2,499 units: 32% discount
    * 2,500 – 4,999 units: 40% discount
    * 5,000+ units: 50% discount
  - Setup Fees & Minimum Order Floor: Setup fees amortized over quantity; orders below
    minimum order value (£15 – £35 depending on division) are automatically rounded up.
  - Tax Math: UK VAT (20.0%) calculated in integer cents to eliminate floating-point errors.

  [MODULE 8] Online Vector Designer Studio & Preflight (components/designer/)
  - Interactive Vector Canvas: Drag, resize, rotate, and align text and vector objects.
  - Typography: Display, Sans-serif, and Monospace font families with dynamic pt sizing.
  - Color Pickers: Brand hex colors, swatch palettes, and stroke/fill controls.
  - Artwork Uploads: Supports PDF, SVG, AI, and PNG file uploads with drag-and-drop.
  - Boundary Guides:
    * Safe Margin Guide: Inner dashed green line (-3mm).
    * Cut Line: Solid border representing finished trim dimensions.
    * Bleed Line: Outer dashed red line (+3mm) for full-bleed printing.
  - Preflight Validation Engine (lib/designer/preflight.ts):
    * DPI Check: Flags uploaded bitmap artwork below 300 DPI.
    * Color Space: Inspects RGB vs. CMYK profiles and recommends FOGRA39 conversion.
    * Bleed Margin Check: Warns when background artwork does not extend to the +3mm bleed edge.

  [MODULE 9] Cryptographic Proof Approval Gate (lib/proofs/)
  - Proof Versioning: Tracks revisions across v1, v2, v3 with change request logs.
  - Digital Signature: Generates SHA-256 digital signature hashes capturing customer ID,
    timestamp, IP address, and proof version upon approval.
  - Authoritative Production File Lock: Production-ready print files return HTTP 423 Locked
    until proof is customer-approved.

  [MODULE 10] Factory Prepress RIP Print Engine (lib/print-engine/pdf-generator.ts)
  - High-Resolution PDF Output: 300 DPI vector PDF generation with FOGRA39 CMYK simulation.
  - Finishing Marks: Automatically adds standard crop marks, bleed lines (+3mm), and job slugs.
  - Production File Naming: Canonical naming format (`PRINT_{ORDER_NUMBER}_{PRODUCT}_{TIMESTAMP}.pdf`).
  - Download Endpoint: `/api/production/download/[orderId]` serving streamable print files
    strictly to authorized staff or for approved orders.

  [MODULE 11] CSV Variable Data Print (VDP) Engine (lib/print-engine/vdp-processor.ts)
  - High-Throughput Batch Processing: Merges up to 5,000 CSV records into production runs.
  - Variable Fields: First Name, Last Name, Bib Number, Category, Emergency Contact.
  - Dynamic Barcode Generation: Automated Code 128 and QR code rendering for timing chips.
  - Typography Protection: Auto-shrink-to-fit text with min font size floors and tracking limits.

  [MODULE 12] Factory Shop-Floor Operations Kanban Pipeline (app/admin/production)
  - Live Drag-and-Drop Kanban: QUOTE → ORDER → ARTWORK → PROOF → PRODUCTION → COMPLETED.
  - Machine Line Allocation: Schedules jobs to specific presses (HP Indigo 12000, Roland TrueVIS,
    Trotec Speedy 400 Laser, Heidelberg Platen).
  - Searchable Archive: Historical order search with date, customer, and division filters.

  [MODULE 13] Admin Back-Office Operations
  - /admin: Executive KPI overview tracking queue volume, pending proofs, and revenue.
  - /admin/customers: Directory of registered clients with account creation modal.
  - /admin/products: Catalog manager to toggle product availability and add new substrates.
  - /admin/pricing: Formula editor for base rates, setup fees, VAT %, and discount curves.
  - /api/admin/orders/[orderId]/refund: One-click refund API updating payments and audit trail.

  [MODULE 14] Transactional Email Engine & Outbox (lib/email/)
  - 4 Branded Responsive Email Templates:
    * ORDER_CONFIRMED: VAT receipt and order summary.
    * PROOF_READY_FOR_REVIEW: Secure link to proof approval gate.
    * PROOF_APPROVED: Production queue notification with SHA-256 signature certificate.
    * JOB_COMPLETED_DISPATCHED: Courier dispatch notice with tracking code.
  - Idempotency Deduplication Gate: 15-minute suppression window preventing duplicate sends.
  - Admin Outbox Viewer: Searchable outbox log with HTML preview and manual staff resend.

  [MODULE 15] UI System & Specificity Collision Resolution
  - components/ui/button.tsx: Smart conflict detection dynamically stripping conflicting
    variant classes (`bg-white`, `text-[#13171C]`) when callers pass custom color classes.
  - variant="dark-outline": First-class dark button variant (`bg-[#1E252D] border-[#384350] text-white`)
    eliminating white-on-white button rendering across all dark admin consoles.
  - Responsive Viewports: Mobile-first responsive layout tested across 320px, 375px, 768px,
    1024px, and 1440px viewports with touch targets >= 44px and zero horizontal overflow.

  [MODULE 16] Supabase PostgreSQL Database & Storage
  - Supabase Project URL: https://jecmqthnenypunwnligs.supabase.co
  - 18 Relational Tables: profiles, product_divisions, products, product_parameters,
    pricing_rules, volume_discounts, templates, designs, artwork_uploads, orders,
    order_items, proof_versions, proof_approvals, production_files, payments, invoices,
    notifications, order_status_history.
  - 5 Custom ENUMs: user_role, pipeline_stage, file_preflight_status, production_file_type, payment_status.
  - 29 Row-Level Security (RLS) Policies: Strict tenant and role isolation.
  - 2 Storage Buckets: `customer-artwork` and `production-files` (verified active).
